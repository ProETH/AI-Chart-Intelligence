# AI Chart Intelligence

AI Chart Intelligence is an explainable AI chart analysis Skill developed for the CoinW AI Trading Skill Challenge.

Unlike traditional technical indicators that simply output numerical values, this Skill interprets the entire price chart and transforms visual market structures into actionable intelligence for AI Agents.

The objective is to help AI Agents understand market behavior, identify high-probability chart structures, evaluate trend quality, and explain every conclusion through transparent AI reasoning.

---

# Why AI Chart Intelligence?

Charts contain much more information than individual technical indicators.

Instead of asking:

> "Is RSI overbought?"

AI Chart Intelligence asks:

- What is the current market structure?
- Is the trend healthy?
- Is this breakout genuine?
- Is liquidity being collected?
- Which pattern is forming?
- How confident is the pattern?

This allows AI Agents to understand price action instead of reacting to isolated indicators.

# Chart Intelligence Modules

## Market Structure

Identifies:

- Higher Highs
- Higher Lows
- Lower Highs
- Lower Lows

to classify market structure.

---

## Trend Analysis

Determines whether the market is:

- Strong Bullish
- Bullish
- Sideways
- Bearish
- Strong Bearish

---

## Pattern Recognition

Recognizes common chart patterns including:

- Ascending Triangle
- Descending Triangle
- Symmetrical Triangle
- Flag
- Pennant
- Channel
- Wedge
- Cup & Handle
- Double Top
- Double Bottom
- Head & Shoulders

Additional patterns can be integrated in future versions.

---

## Breakout Analysis

Evaluates:

- Breakout Quality
- Breakout Strength
- Breakout Probability

---

## Fake Breakout Detection

Detects failed breakouts using price behavior, momentum, and volume confirmation.

---

## Support & Resistance

Automatically identifies important support and resistance zones.

---

## Liquidity Analysis

Detects:

- Liquidity Sweeps
- Stop Hunts
- Rejection Zones

---

## Multi-Timeframe Analysis

Compares chart structures across:

- 15 Minutes
- 1 Hour
- 4 Hours
- 1 Day

and generates a Multi-Timeframe Confluence Score.
