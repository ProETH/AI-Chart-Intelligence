# AI Chart Intelligence

## Skill Overview

AI Chart Intelligence is an explainable AI chart analysis Skill that transforms raw market charts into structured trading intelligence for AI Agents.

Instead of relying on isolated technical indicators, the Skill analyzes price structure, trend behavior, chart patterns, support and resistance zones, liquidity events, and multi-timeframe confluence to generate an explainable Chart Intelligence Report.

Its objective is to help AI Agents understand **what the chart is actually saying**, rather than simply reacting to indicator values.

# Core Strategy Logic

The Skill analyzes historical and real-time price data to understand market structure and identify high-probability trading conditions.

Rather than producing a simple Buy or Sell signal, it evaluates the complete chart context and generates explainable market intelligence.

The AI combines multiple chart analysis modules into a unified confidence score before producing recommendations.

# Risk Notice

AI Chart Intelligence is a chart analysis and decision-support Skill.

Unexpected market events, macroeconomic news, regulatory announcements, exchange incidents, or sudden volatility may rapidly invalidate chart structures and trading patterns.

Users should always combine AI-generated chart intelligence with independent research and proper risk management before making trading decisions.

---

# Parameter Calibration

Chart Intelligence is adaptive rather than rule-based.

Pattern confidence, trend quality, breakout probability, and market structure are continuously calibrated according to changing market behavior instead of relying on fixed thresholds.
